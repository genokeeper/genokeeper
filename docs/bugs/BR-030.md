- **Found:** 2026-11-05 (the first `small_variants` build on the masked GRCh38, on public GIAB data)
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/calibrate/build.py`, `build_table`; `src/genokeeper/calibrate/hg002.py`,
  `hg004_snp_f1`; `src/genokeeper/calibrate/hg002_cells.py`, `lab_regions`;
  `src/genokeeper/calibrate/foundation.py`, `build_foundation`.

HG003's `small_variants` table, scored against GIAB's v4.2.1 truth on the masked reference,
failed its chr1-19 -> chr20-22 held-out check: 7 of 66 cells outside (10.6%, limit 10%), against
5 of 66 on the unmasked reference. The benchmark regions lost the masked copies of the GRC false
duplications but kept the correct copies. The truth was made on the unmasked reference, where
reads of a falsely duplicated haplotype could go to the false copy, so it can hold no variant
on the copy that stays. On the masked reference all those reads land there and DeepVariant
calls real heterozygotes the truth lacks.

**Evidence.** 18 of the 19 chr21 difficult-SNV genotype errors at GQ 40+ sit in two clusters,
chr21:44,146,986-44,153,478 and chr21:44,224,621-44,232,761, all 0/1 false positives with no such
allele in the truth, DP 32-51. Aligned with bwa, every 1 kb chunk of chr21:44,140,000-44,240,000
of the unmasked FASTA maps (MAPQ 60, inverted) onto the masked block chr21:5,010,000-5,166,246,
and in all 18 positions the called ALT equals the masked copy's base.

**What this evidence does not establish:** that every correct copy carries such errors. Only
chr21's were read. The other eleven are left out because the truth's construction is the same
there, not because their errors were counted. Nor is the v5.0q truth's behaviour on these copies
measured: it is left out with the rest on the owner's ruling.

## Fix

`genokeeper/genokeeper#17`. GIAB's v3.6 correct-copy BED is committed as
`resources/reference/false_duplications_correct_copy.GRCh38.tsv`, and the four readers above
leave it out as well as the mask. Its sha256 is a seventh `VERSION_KEYS` entry, `correct_copies`,
in `calibration_versions` and `foundation_versions`, so `small_variants`, `hg002_cells` and
`foundation` read stale and a run computes the same value.

Covered by these tests, each failing with its fix reverted:
- `tests/test_pipeline_invariants.py`'s `test_calibrate_writes_a_table_the_run_accepts` and
  `test_small_variant_tables_stale_when_the_correct_copies_change`;
- `tests/test_calibrate_hg002.py`'s `test_accept_hg004_at_f1`;
- `tests/test_hg002_cells.py`'s `test_lab_regions_leave_out_the_correct_copies`;
- `tests/test_calibrate_foundation.py`'s `test_foundation_true_hets_leave_out_the_correct_copies`;
- `tests/test_masking.py`'s `test_without_false_duplications_drops_both_copies` and
  `test_correct_copies_are_giabs_file`.

Deliberately not changed:
- the structural, CNV, insertion, paralog, stage 3 and slice 3f measurements, whose versions do
  not move;
- HG004's stored SNP F1, which only `hg002 accept hg004` writes;
- the run's grading and gene coverage, which still read HG003's whole benchmark BED, so a site on
  a correct copy is graded by the table though the table is no longer measured there.

The evidence boundary stands. The held-out check after this change is not measured until the
table is rebuilt.
