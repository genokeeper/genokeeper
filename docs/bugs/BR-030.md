- **Found:** 2026-11-05 (the first `small_variants` build on the masked GRCh38, on public GIAB data)
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/calibrate/build.py`, `build_table` and `_measure_library`'s ROH;
  `src/genokeeper/calibrate/hg002.py`, `hg004_snp_f1`; at run time `src/genokeeper/pipeline.py`, `open_calibration` and
  `foundation_steps`.

HG003's `small_variants` table, scored against GIAB's v4.2.1 truth on the masked reference,
failed its chr1-19 -> chr20-22 held-out check: 7 of 66 cells outside (10.6%, limit 10%), against
5 of 66 on the unmasked reference. The benchmark regions lost the masked copies of the GRC false
duplications but kept the correct copies. The truth was made on the unmasked reference, where
reads of a falsely duplicated haplotype could go to the false copy, so it can hold no variant
on the copy that stays. On the masked reference all those reads land there and DeepVariant
calls real heterozygotes the truth lacks.

**Evidence.** 18 of the 19 chr21 difficult-SNV genotype errors at GQ 40+ sit in two clusters,
chr21:44,146,986-44,153,478 and chr21:44,224,621-44,232,761, all 0/1 false positives with no such
allele in the truth, DP 32-51. Aligned with bwa, every 1 kb chunk of chr21:44,140,000-44,240,000
of the unmasked FASTA maps (MAPQ 60, inverted) onto the masked block chr21:5,010,000-5,166,246,
and in all 18 positions the called ALT equals the masked copy's base.

Whether v5.0q is valid there was measured on public samples, DeepVariant on the masked reference,
over GIAB's 12 correct-copy intervals, with GQ 40 as the high band
(`.superpowers/lab/heldout-v5-copies.out`, git-ignored):

| | HG003, v4.2.1 | HG002 PCR-free, v5.0q |
|---|---|---|
| Copy bases in the benchmark | 693,695 of 1,237,625 | 894,640 of 1,237,625 |
| Calls scored on the copies | 503 | 1,431 |
| High GQ errors on the copies | 20 of 351 | 0 of 789 |
| Low GQ errors on the copies | 11 of 152 | 28 of 642 |
| High GQ difficult, chr20-22 control | SNV 1 of 17,877, indel 1 of 10,238 | SNV 26 of 15,211, indel 45 of 13,421 |
| Low GQ difficult, chr20-22 control | SNV 2.40%, indel 0.94% | SNV 4.60%, indel 6.49% |

All 20 of HG003's high-GQ errors are in chr21:44,095,806-44,253,497. In that interval HG002 has
3 errors in 358 calls, none at GQ 40+. So the same check fails on the known-bad truth and shows no
excess on v5.0q.

v5.0q holds none of chr13:111,668,941-111,703,855, chr16:34,955,067-35,072,499 and
chr22:18,774,319-18,939,751, and 2,997 of the 17,992 bp of chr9:70,719,799-70,737,791. The coding
blocks of CBS (1,653 bp), CRYAA (519), KCNE1 (387) and U2AF1 (720), the reference-limited genes in
`resources/coverage/cds.GRCh38.tsv`, all lie on the copies and all inside v5.0q; no other gene in
that file has a coding block on a copy.

**What this evidence does not establish:** that v5.0q is correct on the copies. 0 of 789 is a small
sample: at the control's rates about 1.6 errors would be expected, which leaves a 21% chance of
none, so it shows no excess and cannot show a lower rate. The two columns are different samples,
and HG002 may have been in DeepVariant's training, the reason the shared part exists. Only
chr21's HG003 errors were traced to a false copy; the other eleven copies leave v4.2.1 because
the truth's construction is the same there, not because errors were counted on each.

## Fix

`genokeeper/genokeeper#17`. GIAB's v3.6 correct-copy BED is committed as
`resources/reference/false_duplications_correct_copy.GRCh38.tsv`.

- HG003's `small_variants`, HG004's SNP F1 and slice 3f's ROH on both, the v4.2.1 measurements,
  leave it out as well as the mask. No v4.2.1 path scores the copies: slice 3f's other reads of
  those truths are listed sites, and none of RBCeq2's 1,055 positions, the six DQ tags or
  UGT1A1's site lies on a copy.
- HG002's v5.0q measurements keep it. `lab_regions` reads HG003's benchmark without the copies, so
  they fall in the `only` part; `build_foundation`'s true heterozygotes are as they were.
- At run time `open_calibration` and the coverage step read HG003's benchmark without the copies.
  A site on a copy inside v5.0q is rated by HG002's cell, and any other site on a copy reads
  `OUTSIDE_CALIBRATED`, as all of them do while the cells are not current.
- Its sha256 is a seventh `VERSION_KEYS` entry, `correct_copies`, so `small_variants` and
  `hg002_cells` read stale and a run computes the same value. The coverage step's key holds it,
  and so does HG003's and HG004's ROH step; slice 3f's `MEASURE_VERSION` is 3.
- The fixed text beside a rate now reads "measured on GIAB reference samples, DNBSEQ-T7": it was
  "measured on HG003" for every call, HG002's cells included, and `Reliability` names no source.

Covered by these tests, each failing with its fix reverted:
- `tests/test_pipeline_invariants.py`: `test_calibrate_writes_a_table_the_run_accepts`,
  `test_a_site_on_a_correct_copy_is_rated_by_hg002_cells`,
  `test_gene_coverage_reads_the_benchmark_the_run_grades_with`,
  `test_coverage_runs_again_when_the_correct_copies_change`,
  `test_small_variant_tables_stale_when_the_correct_copies_change`;
- `tests/test_calibrate_hg002.py`: `test_accept_hg004_at_f1`;
- `tests/test_calibrate_slice3f.py`: `test_roh_leaves_the_correct_copies_out_of_the_v4_truths`;
- `tests/test_hg002_cells.py`: `test_lab_regions_keep_the_correct_copies_in_the_only_part`;
- `tests/test_masking.py`: `test_without_false_duplications_drops_both_copies`,
  `test_correct_copies_are_giabs_file`.

Deliberately not changed:
- the structural, CNV, insertion, paralog, stage 3 and foundation versions;
- `store/schema.sql`'s comment on `p_wrong`, which still says HG003: changing that file owes a
  schema version;
- HG004's stored SNP F1, which only `hg002 accept hg004` writes.

No table was built here. The rebuild of 2026-11-06 measured the held-out check at 2 of 66 and 6 of
66 cells outside (limit 10%), so the miss of 7 of 66 is gone, narrowly. The evidence boundary
stands: more HG002 calls on the copies would be needed to bound their rate.
