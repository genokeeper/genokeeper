- **Found:** 2026-11-02
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/pipeline.py`, `deletion_steps` and the `except` list its
  steps fail through.

A deletion step that fails is meant to leave the screen unassessed, with the reason, while
the run goes on. The concern: a `ToolMissing` (bcftools not installed), `_cached`'s "wrote
no …" `RuntimeError` or an `OSError` (a full disk) might escape that `except` list and abort
the whole `genokeeper run`, every other source's results included.

**Evidence.** To be reproduced: a caller step and the union step, each made to raise each
of the three.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** catch the three where the steps' failures are recorded.
Must not change: Docker unavailable or an image missing still refuses the run
(`RunRefused`).

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether any of the three happens in practice.
