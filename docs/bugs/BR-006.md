- **Found:** 2026-11-02
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/pipeline.py`, `deletion_steps` and the `except` list its
  steps fail through.

A deletion step that fails is meant to leave the screen unassessed, with the reason, while
the run goes on. The concern: a `ToolMissing` (bcftools not installed), `_cached`'s "wrote
no …" `RuntimeError` or an `OSError` (a full disk) might escape that `except` list and abort
the whole `genokeeper run`, every other source's results included.

**Evidence.** Checked as the Fix section shows: a caller step and the union step,
each made to raise each of the three.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** catch the three where the steps' failures are recorded.
Must not change: Docker unavailable or an image missing still refuses the run
(`RunRefused`).

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether any of the three happens in practice.

## Fix

Rejected: it does not reproduce. Every deletion step fails through `_attempt`, whose `except`
list already holds `ToolFailed`, `ToolMissing`, `ValueError`, `KeyError`, `OSError` and
`RuntimeError` (`_cached`'s "wrote no" among them), after `DockerUnavailable` and
`RunRefused`, which still refuse the run. The new test raises each of the three in a caller
step (`deletions_only`) and in the union (`match_callers`); on `origin/main` at f81626d all six
cases already pass:

```
== origin/main f81626d, test_deletion_steps_failure_leaves_the_screen_unassessed
====================== 6 passed, 27 deselected in 14.63s =======================
```

`tests/test_calibrate_deletions.py::test_deletion_steps_failure_leaves_the_screen_unassessed`
stays, as no test held the three before.
