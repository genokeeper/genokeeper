- **Found:** 2026-11-09
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/annotate/vep.py`, `vep_command` (the REVEL plugin's default match) and
  `src/genokeeper/evidence/predicted.py`, `predicted_rule`

VEP's REVEL plugin reports a score only when the row's transcript is among those the REVEL row names
(`/plugins/REVEL.pm` in the pinned image, `sub run`, read). REVEL v1.3 names older Ensembl
transcripts, and many MANE Select transcripts are newer, so the row the grading reads has no REVEL.
`predicted_rule` then uses AlphaMissense at its own threshold. No variant is lost, but whether REVEL
or AlphaMissense decides a missense depends on the age of its transcript's identifier.

Evidence, public data only (`revel_v1.3_grch38.tsv.gz`, MANE v1.5's GTF, AlphaMissense's hg38 file;
scripts run in the session on 2026-11-09):

- 2,978 of 19,359 MANE Select transcripts are named by no REVEL row, 5,498,268 of 33,575,759 of
  their CDS bases; 17 of the 190 listed genes (LDLR, TTN, COL7A1, PCDH15 among them) and 370 of
  2,782 PanelApp panel genes in that file.
- Over those 17 genes, of 344,936 possible changes AlphaMissense scores, 14,823 stand at or above
  REVEL's 0.773 and below AlphaMissense's 0.906 (a finding only by REVEL), 31,690 the reverse.
- A plain `no_match=1` is not the fix. It compares only the new residue (`REVEL.pm`) and takes the
  first row, and at one allele the rows of different reading frames are different protein changes.
  One allele and new residue has several rows in 209,878 adjacent pairs, 207,435 with different
  scores, 15,468 across 0.773 (chr1:943343 T>G: V>G 0.907 on ENST00000342066, W>G 0.350 on
  ENST00000341065).

**What this evidence does not establish:** how many of one genome's findings move. The counts are
of possible changes, not carried ones.

## Fix

`annotate.vep.revel_by_allele` gives a missense VEP left without REVEL the score of its allele and
protein change (the residue replaced and the new one) on any transcript, when every such row agrees,
else none, so AlphaMissense still decides there. A score VEP matched on the transcript stays. The source level says `(from another
transcript)`. `VEP_STEP_VERSION` is 7. Covered by `tests/test_vep.py::test_revel_by_allele`,
`test_revel_by_allele_leaves_the_others`, `test_annotate_fills_revel_by_allele` and
`tests/test_predicted.py::test_a_revel_score_from_another_transcript_says_so`. On the pinned VEP image,
three public variants: `LDLR` and `ACTB` took REVEL by allele, `BRCA1`'s stayed on its transcript.
Not changed: REVEL first, AlphaMissense only where REVEL has no score; the thresholds. So a score
read by allele below 0.773 decides over the transcript's AlphaMissense, and such a finding leaves the
run with no statement to carry the mark: the owner chose the one rule on 2026-11-09, shown both
directions.
Left alone: a MANE Plus Clinical row of equal rank and another protein change adds no record
(`parse_vep`), which reaches 11 possible changes genome-wide (MOCS2, FXR1).
