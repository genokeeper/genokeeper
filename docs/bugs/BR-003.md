- **Found:** 2026-11-02
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/deletions/screen.py`, `screen` and `is_common`.

A deletion common in gnomAD-SV (PASS, AN at least 2000, AF above 1%, reciprocal overlap at
least 0.5) is dropped as `COMMON` in every gene. The small-variant path keeps a list of BA1
exceptions for common pathogenic alleles (`resources/ba1_exceptions.tsv`); the deletion
path has none. In a recessive carrier gene a common deletion can be a real carrier allele,
and the screen would then hide it.

**Evidence.** By reading the code: `screen` returns `COMMON` before any gene is consulted,
and nothing in `deletions/` reads an exception list. Whether any listed gene is affected
needs the common records themselves, which the next section's cross-check counts.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** a deletion-exceptions list beside `ba1_exceptions`, each
entry cited, applied before the `COMMON` test. Must not change: the test for every other
deletion. Alternative: no list, if no common gnomAD-SV deletion over the targets is a
pathogenic allele the screen could show.

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether any common gnomAD-SV deletion over a
listed gene's exons is a known pathogenic allele; that needs gnomAD-SV's common deletions
over the targets, checked one by one. Settled by the cross-check in the Fix section, for
gnomAD-SV v4.1.

## Fix

Rejected: no pathogenic allele the screen could show is dropped. The cross-check the entry
asked for, run on gnomAD-SV v4.1 (public data; a script over `bcftools query -R` on the
targets, its output kept), finds ten common records over the targets:

```
RB1	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr13_eade0d74	chr13:48307327-48308381	1054 bp	AF 0.019	manta,wham
HBA1,HBA2	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr16_e447ef44	chr16:172000-177475	5475 bp	AF 0.055	depth
ABCA3	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr16_f3f7f0bc	chr16:2297901-2298189	288 bp	AF 0.117	manta,wham
CBS	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr21_ff594b43	chr21:43036196-43054392	18196 bp	AF 0.215	depth
CBS	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr21_67ca8d99	chr21:43046392-43172392	126000 bp	AF 0.468	depth
MLC1	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr22_3f9396c4	chr22:50060226-50060799	573 bp	AF 0.120	manta,wham
TF	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr3_cb906bcb	chr3:133784485-133786273	1788 bp	AF 0.022	wham
TF	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr3_a5f3cbbd	chr3:133784938-133786238	1300 bp	AF 0.299	manta
SMN1	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr5_acdeb328	chr5:70793200-70985807	192607 bp	AF 0.071	depth
SMN1	gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr5_a8ebfa25	chr5:70952653-70963307	10654 bp	AF 0.028	depth
```

The one known pathogenic allele among them is the depth-only record over HBA2 and HBA1, by
its place, length and frequency the -α3.7 deletion (its breakpoints are depth bins); every
run already states -α3.7 and -α4.2 not covered (`sources/paralogs.py`, `NOT_COVERED`), so an
exception would show a call in a gene the run says it cannot read. SMN1's copy number is
slice 3a's, from SMNCopyNumberCaller and Parascopy. The rest fail by frequency alone,
under Hardy-Weinberg: a recessive allele at frequency q leaves q² of births affected, so
ABCA3's (0.117) and MLC1's (0.120) would make about 1.4% of births affected, TF's (0.299 and
0.022) 9% and 1 in 2,000, CBS's (0.215 and 0.468) 4.6% and 22%, against diseases of a few
reported families (atransferrinaemia) or of 1 in several thousand births or rarer; RB1's, a
dominant gene's, at 0.019 would give retinoblastoma to about 4% of people. CBS's two are
depth-only calls over the real copy of a region GRCh38 also holds a false copy of (at
chr21:5.0-7.9 Mb, which slice 3c masks for this pipeline's own alignment, not gnomAD-SV's),
most likely artefacts of that duplication.

Not changed: the `COMMON` test. Its cost here is that a true CBS deletion overlapping
either CBS record (18 kb or 126 kb) by half or more both ways would be dropped as common; no record over a target is a
carrier allele the screen could show. If a later release adds one, the list this entry
suggested is the fix, cited per entry.
