- **Found:** 2026-11-02 (the second independent review of `genokeeper/genokeeper#10`)
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/bloodgroups.py`, `uncovered` (R10's no-call guard), as read by
  `src/genokeeper/sources/bloodgroups.py`, `BloodGroupSource._kind`, and by
  `src/genokeeper/calibrate/build.py`, `_systems` (ours and the truth's).

R10's guard makes a system unassessed when one of its own written positions is a no-call,
because RBCeq2 reads a no-call as the reference. RBCeq2 2.4.4 also builds three systems from
another system's alleles. Read in its image, offline:
- `main` writes FUT1's and FUT2's genotype pairs into each other's genotype.
- `choose_pheno.FUT1` joins FUT2's phenotype to FUT1's (HG002: `H+,Se-`).
- `choose_pheno.FUT3` takes an active FUT3's Lewis type from FUT2's secretor status: Se+ gives
  Le(a-b+), Se- gives Le(a+b-), Se+w gives Le(a+b+).

No other system reads another's alleles. So with a no-call at a FUT2 position, FUT2 reads
unknown, but FUT1 still states a secretor status and FUT3 a Lewis type, both read from the
reference there. BR-026's two FUT2 statements are then contradicted by statements nothing
measured. A no-call at a FUT1 position likewise leaves FUT2's genotype carrying FUT1 alleles
read from the reference. The calibration's `_systems` has the same gap, for our calls and
for the truth's. It shows at validity limited once slice 3f is calibrated; the default view
hides every blood group.

**Evidence.** The fix's tests, run before the fix (the test run's last lines; the full
output is kept in the git-ignored `.superpowers/br029/before.txt`):

```

FAILED tests/test_bloodgroups.py::test_calibration_reads_the_same_dependencies

```

The calibration test's failure shows `_systems(HG002, {"FUT2": (48703417,)})["FUT1"]` as
`SystemCall(genotype='FUT1*01/FUT1*01,FUT2*01N.02/FUT2*01N.02', phenotype='H+,Se-')`, not
None.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** a dependency map, `DEPENDS = {FUT1: FUT2, FUT2: FUT1,
FUT3: FUT2}`, applied where `uncovered` is read: by the source, whose reason names the other
system in words, and by the calibration's `_systems`. The map goes into the truth step's
cache key, since `_systems` runs before that step writes. Must not change: the cached typing
step's `uncovered`, and every system outside the three. Considered: mapping only FUT1←FUT2
and FUT3←FUT2 and trimming the cross-written pairs from the genotype strings; and always
dropping FUT1's Se part and FUT3's derived Lewis type. Both parse RBCeq2's output grammar,
and the second loses Lewis on every run.

**What this evidence does not establish:** how often a FUT1 or FUT2 position is a no-call
on 30x DNBSEQ data. Under the fix, a no-call at any of FUT1's 75 positions also leaves FUT2
unassessed, and its cost is unmeasured until a run's no-call counts per system are read.
