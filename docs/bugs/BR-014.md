- **Found:** 2026-11-02, carried over from the private history's ledger (a 2026-10-23
  implementer's report, by reading), reproduced on this tree.
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/evidence/predicted.py`, `truncations_3prime`.

The gate-3 rescue counts ClinVar P/LP truncating variants of the gene whose genomic position lies
between the escaping stop and the transcript's CDS end. It does not check that they lie in the
stop transcript's coding bases: for a stop within 50 nt of the last junction the span crosses the
last intron, so a truncation in that intron (one on an exon only another transcript has) counts
as "3' of the stop", and two of them rescue a stop the decision tree's critical-region step
would not. The cost is a false finding: a predicted LoF placed under risks.

**Evidence.** A scratch test on `main` (f81626d), with the test module's BRCA1 fixture (MANE
transcript on +, escape regions 15951-16000 and 20001-20400): a stop-gain at CDS 1063
(genomic 15963, `escape` rule TRACK) and two ClinVar truncations at 16001 and 18000, both in the
last intron. It printed `COUNT 2 risks predicted, not classified; ...`: both counted, the stop
rescued, the grade a finding under risks.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** count only truncations inside the track's escape regions of
the stop's transcript, which are that transcript's last coding bases, and 3' of the stop by the
regions' strand. Must not change: the two-variant threshold, the rule that a stop in the first
100 coding bases is never rescued. Alternative: exclude splice-site consequences from the count,
which leaves the other-transcript case open.

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether any real rescue depends on such a variant.

## Fix

`truncations_3prime` now takes the stop transcript's escape regions (UCSC's NMD Escape track for
MANE, the transcript's last coding bases) and counts a ClinVar truncation only when it lies in
one of them and 3' of the stop by the regions' strand. A truncation in the last intron, which on
the stop's transcript can only be another transcript's exon, no longer counts. `Escape.cds_end`
is gone: it had no reader once the regions bound the count. Covered by
`tests/test_predicted.py::test_rescue_counts_only_the_stop_transcripts_coding_bases` (two
intronic truncations give 0 and the stop stays under research; two in the regions give 2, on
either strand) and the existing
`test_escaping_stop_rescued_by_two_downstream_clinvar_truncations`. Not changed: the
two-variant threshold, a stop in the first 100 coding bases is never rescued, and the count
reads ClinVar's gene symbol as before.
