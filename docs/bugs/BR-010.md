- **Found:** 2026-10-28 (slice 3a's re-review), carried over from the private archive's
  ledger and reproduced on `main` at `f81626d` on 2026-11-02.
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/dashboard.py` (`payload`) and `src/genokeeper/explain.py`, both
  building from `query()`.

`query()` returns findings and unassessed claims, so the dashboard and `explain` show no
stage-3 watch item (an intermediate or borderline repeat, an SMN1 silent-carrier marker) and
no stage-3 non-finding ("HTT normal", "SMN1 two copies"). Slice 3f's "Excluded by screening"
block lists only the non-findings flagged `exclusion` (the exclusion-only loci's normals), and
the not-covered list reads the stage-3 kinds only to drop a gene from it. The stage-3 design's
acceptance asks that the dashboard, `explain` and genome-ask show SMN, the repeat loci and the
Y haplogroup, each graded. Today the owner sees a watch item only through the genome-ask skill
(`genotype`, `show`).

**Evidence.** `tests/test_dashboard.py::test_screened_lists_stage3_watch_items_and_non_findings_apart`
on `main` at `f81626d` before any fix (pytest's output): the payload holds no such list.

```
E       KeyError: 'screened'
FAILED tests/test_dashboard.py::test_screened_lists_stage3_watch_items_and_non_findings_apart
```

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** a block on the dashboard fed by the run's stage-3
gene-level statements of the two missing kinds (the sources `carrier.STAGE3_SOURCES` names),
a watch item apart from a non-finding, each with its stored reason. Must not change: a
non-finding never reads as a finding, a watch item never as a normal, and a flagged normal
stays in the "Excluded by screening" block alone. Alternative: watch items added to
`explain`'s list, which would ask the model to explain a watch item with instructions
written for a finding.

**What this evidence does not establish:** which stage-3 kinds the owner wants on the
dashboard; that is a product choice.

## Fix

`dashboard._screened` gives the payload's `screened`: the run's gene-level watch items and
non-findings from `carrier.STAGE3_SOURCES`, not flagged `exclusion`, each with its gene and
gene call's diplotype and its stored reason (else its gene call's phenotype). The page shows
them under the "Excluded by screening" block as two lists, "To watch: neither normal nor a
finding" and "Screened, not a finding", in English and Russian
(`resources/dashboard/messages.json`); the README says so.
`tests/test_dashboard.py::test_screened_lists_stage3_watch_items_and_non_findings_apart`.

Not changed: the feed, its views and counts; a flagged normal stays in the "Excluded by
screening" block alone. `explain` is not extended: its instructions explain a finding, and
asking them to explain a watch item would word it as one; the block's stored reason already
says what each item is.
