- **Found:** 2026-11-06 (`genokeeper calibrate --only slice3f` on HG002 PCR-free)
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/prs/pgsc.py`, `write_target_vcf`;
  `src/genokeeper/pipeline.py`, `build_admixture` in `typing_steps`.

The panel stores a position with several ALTs as biallelic rows, so `--max-alleles 2` in
`build_panel_reference` keeps them. `write_target_vcf` puts the ALTs of one position on one row,
the shape pgsc_calc takes. plink2's `--make-bed` refuses that row, and the step's name made the
message read as fastmixture's:

```
fastmixture failed: exit 7: Error: /out/target.bim cannot contain multiallelic variants.
```

`tests/fixtures/genome_mini.py` did not catch it because `FakeTyping` gave one allele and replaced
`to_bed` with a stub.

**Evidence.** The public `pruned.bim`: 417,469 rows, 599 positions with more than one row (532
with two, 67 with three), every pair adjacent with one REF. No other caller is affected: pgsc_calc
takes the merged row, and ROH's `bcftools roh` passed on it.

**What this evidence does not establish:** what the projection gains or loses on real samples. The
synthetic person proves the bed's shape and fastmixture's acceptance, not its proportions. The
panel's own encoding of a person with two ALTs at a split position was not read, so a GT `1/2`
written as `0/1` on both rows may not match how the P file counted such a person.

## Fix

`write_target_vcf(..., one_row_per_alt=True)` keeps one row per `pruned.bim` row, which is also
what fastmixture's projection needs: as many rows as the P file. A person who carries another ALT
of the position and none of this one reads `./.` on this ALT's row where the true dosage is 0, on
at most 1,265 of 417,469 rows, and only in a carrier: it costs a site and states no wrong ALT
dosage. A GT `1/2` with both ALTs listed reads `0/1` on both rows. The other callers' files stay byte for byte the same. The fixture's fake now refuses such a row and lists two ALTs at one
position. A synthetic person through the real plink2 and fastmixture on the real P file gave
417,469 bim rows in `pruned.bim`'s order and a projection.
