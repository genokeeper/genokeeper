- **Found:** 2026-11-02, carried over from the private history's ledger (a 2026-10-23 review
  finding, read but not run).
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/sources/promote.py`, `_hets`, which builds the per-gene list
  `candidate_pairs` pairs.

The claim: in a carrier Tier 3 gene, a heterozygous ClinVar P/LP carrier finding and a
heterozygous predicted carrier finding (`sources.predicted`) never share a gene list, so the two
are never phased as a possible compound heterozygote; in trans they would mean "probably
affected", and the user reads "carrier" twice instead.

**Evidence.** Not yet observed on this tree; to be settled by a test with one ClinVar carrier
finding and one predicted carrier finding in CFTR (a Tier 3 gene) through `candidate_pairs`.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** put both kinds of carrier finding in one candidate list. Must
not change: pairs of two ClinVar rows, haploid loci, the `dosage == "1"` filter. Alternative: say
in the gene answer that a mixed pair exists, unphased.

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether the defect still exists after the 2026-10-28
pairing decision. Settled below: it does not.

## Fix

Rejected: it does not reproduce on this tree. `_hets` admits a statement as a carrier het by its
kind and topic alone (`kind == finding and topic == carrier`), whatever its source, and
`evidence.clinvar` grades a heterozygous P/LP claim in a carrier gene a carrier finding, so a
ClinVar and a predicted carrier finding in one Tier 3 gene share a gene list. A scratch test on
`main` (f81626d) with one ClinVar carrier finding and one predicted carrier finding in CFTR
through `candidate_pairs` returned the pair and passed. The behaviour is already pinned by
`tests/test_phasing.py::test_tier3_and_predicted_pairs_promoted` (a classified and a predicted
carrier het pair in MUTYH, on the same source-agnostic branch); no test or code was added.
