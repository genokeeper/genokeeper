- **Found:** 2026-11-02, carried over from the private history's ledger (a 2026-10-23 review
  finding), reproduced on this tree.
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/annotate/vep.py`, `parse_vep` (one record per variant by one key
  over all its rows) and `annotate` (SpliceAI's scores matched by `(vid, symbol)`).

`parse_vep` keeps one row per variant. A MANE Select row with a consequence of interest wins;
otherwise consequence rank decides before MANE. So when a gene's MANE Select row is intronic and
its MANE Plus Clinical row is a missense, a stop-gain on a minor isoform (or in an overlapping
gene) keeps the record: it fails gate 2 (not MANE) and goes under research, and the MANE Plus
Clinical missense is never graded against REVEL or AlphaMissense. When the winning row is
another gene's, SpliceAI also skips the variant, because the record's gene is no longer a listed
one. The cost is a missed finding, never a false one.

Second part, same entry: SpliceAI's scores are matched by `(vid, VEP's SYMBOL)`, but SpliceAI's
bundled GRCh38 annotation names genes as GENCODE did in 2016, so a listed gene renamed since
(MMUT, ELP1, PLPBP, CFAP410, DNAAF11, DYNC2I2 and POMGNT2 are on the lists) gets no score.

**Evidence.** A scratch test on `main` (f81626d) wrote three VEP rows for one variant in BRCA1:
the MANE Select transcript `intron_variant`, the MANE Plus Clinical transcript
`missense_variant` (REVEL 0.95), a third transcript `stop_gained`. `parse_vep(path,
keep={"BRCA1"})` printed `('GRCh38:chr1:1100:C:T', 'BRCA1', 'ENST03', 'stop_gained')`: the minor
isoform's stop. The renamed genes were listed by intersecting `load_gene_lists()` with symbols
renamed by HGNC since GENCODE v24; the SpliceAI part is by reading, not run.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** stop choosing one row per variant: keep, per (variant, gene),
every MANE row whose consequence the grading reads, else the gene's best row; grade each. Must
not change: the LoF gates, a minor isoform's LoF never hides a MANE missense (design decision,
2026-10-28), a predicted finding never outranks a classified one. Alternative: keep one record
but grade the MANE row whenever the chosen row fails gate 2. For the rename: fall back to the
variant's only SpliceAI entry when no entry carries VEP's symbol.

**What this evidence does not establish:** how often this happens on a real genome.

## Fix

Partial: the row choice is fixed, the SpliceAI rename is not.

The seam was redesigned with BR-013's: `parse_vep` no longer chooses one row per variant. It
gives records per variant and Ensembl gene: each gene's MANE Select row of interest, its MANE
Plus Clinical row of a higher rank, else the gene's row of the highest rank. The MANE Plus
Clinical missense of the evidence is now graded, and a minor isoform's stop is dropped beside
it, as the 2026-10-28 decision says for MANE Select. SpliceAI is given every variant with a
record in a listed gene, whatever another gene's rows. `VEP_STEP_VERSION` is 5, so a cached
annotation is made again. Covered by `tests/test_vep.py::test_vep_records_per_gene`
(`PLUS_MISSENSE`, and `OVERLAP_MANE_STOP`, where another gene's MANE Select stop no longer hides
the listed gene's record, which SpliceAI is then given). Not changed: the LoF gates,
the rank order, a predicted finding never outranks a classified one.

Still open: SpliceAI's scores are matched by `(vid, VEP's symbol)`, so a listed gene renamed
since SpliceAI's 2016 annotation gets no score. A fallback to the variant's only SpliceAI entry
was tried and dropped in review: where a listed gene has only an up- or downstream row and
SpliceAI scores the overlapping gene alone, that entry is the other gene's, and its score could
make a false splice finding in the listed gene. A fix needs the old symbols of the listed genes
(HGNC's previous symbols) or a SpliceAI annotation built from VEP's own GENCODE release.
