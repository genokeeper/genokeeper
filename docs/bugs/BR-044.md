- **Found:** 2026-11-14, by querying the packaged HG002 0.2.0 demo store (public GIAB data)
- **Where:**
  - `src/genokeeper/deletions/callers.py`, `depth_ratio` (DELLY's inside read count over its two
    flanks', taken over the whole call);
  - `src/genokeeper/sources/deletions.py`, `DeletionSource._gene_rows`: the
    `depth_decides_from` rule withholds a call of 1 Mb or more only at
    `ratio >= max_depth_ratio` (0.7).

The depth rule (PR #55) withholds 24 of HG002's 25 shown calls of 1 Mb or more. The one that
stays spans chr1:95.7-148.4 Mb, across the centromere and 1q12, at a depth ratio of 0.69, just
under 0.7. About 20 Mb of that span cannot be mapped, so the ratio is taken partly over bases no
read covers, which alone pulls it down (the reviewer's arithmetic puts that at 0.6-0.7 with
normal depth elsewhere; not measured). The call lands in the `delly+depth` class (measured 81%
false, against 99.7% for `delly` alone) and gives a whole-gene DPYD deletion under Drugs,
beside PharmCAT's normal DPYD. Captured with the store query saved as
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-14-cleanup/br-chr1-hg002-0.2.0.txt` (git-ignored):

```
deletions:DPYD:chr1:95689022-148409719 finding drugs 0.6892106813611983 {'band': 'long', 'evidence': 'delly+depth', 'repeat': False, 'masked': True} possible deletion, …
```

A second, related point from the same review: the deletion table's `long:delly` rate (110
false, 5 true on the PCR-free library) was measured with the megabase calls in, and is still
applied to the calls under 1 Mb that the rule leaves, a different population. The direction is
conservative (the rate overstates how often they are false).

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** compute the ratio over the call's mappable bases only (the
masked regions the screen already loads), or treat a megabase call whose span is mostly masked
as unsupported. Must not change: the ratio of calls under `depth_decides_from`, where depth is
one feature among the class's, and the withheld value kept under `claim.withheld`.
Alternatives considered: a stricter threshold for megabase calls (a chosen number with no
measurement behind it); dropping `masked` megabase calls outright (would also drop a real
deletion near a segmental duplication). At the next `calibrate --only deletions`, measure the
`long` classes with the megabase rule applied.

**What this evidence does not establish:** that the chr1 call is false. Only that its ratio is
taken over unmappable bases; the depth over its mappable part was not measured, and a read-depth
caller's view of the same span (CNVpytor, DELLY cnv) was not looked at.
