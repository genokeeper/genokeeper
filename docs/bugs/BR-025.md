- **Found:** 2026-11-02, the `explain` half of BR-010, deferred when its dashboard half was
  fixed; reproduced on `main` at `8a88393`.
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/explain.py` (`_explain_command`, `prompt`) and
  `src/genokeeper/dashboard.py` (`payload`'s `screened`).

`genokeeper explain` asks for the findings `query()` returns, and `query()` holds no watch
item, so a stage-3 watch item (a borderline or intermediate repeat, an SMN1 silent-carrier
marker) never gets the plain "what it means" a finding gets. Since BR-010 the dashboard lists
these watch items under the feed with their call and stored reason, which name the result
("screening result, needs confirmation: borderline, indeterminate") without saying what it
means for the reader. The stage-3 design asks that the dashboard, `explain` and genome-ask
show them. The instructions `explain` sends were written for "one finding", with a carrier
finding as the example, and their facts carry no kind, so a watch item cannot simply be added
to the list: the model would word it as a finding.

**Evidence.** `tests/test_explain.py::test_explain_asks_for_a_stage3_watch_item_as_a_watch_item`
on `main` at `8a88393` before any fix (pytest's output), on a fixture store with one stage-3
watch item:

```
E       AssertionError: assert {'clinvar:1',...rmcat:CYP2D6'} == {'clinvar:1',...rmcat:CYP2D6'}
E         Extra items in the right set:
E         'expansionhunter:HTT'
```

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** `explain` also asks for the run's stage-3 gene-level watch
items, with one more instruction line telling the model the item is a watch item, neither
normal nor a finding, never to be worded as a finding, an expansion or a diagnosis; the
dashboard's watch list shows the cached explanation. Must not change: a finding's prompt,
byte for byte, so no cached explanation's key moves. Not covered: stage-3 non-findings, whose
call and stored reason already say "normal" or "not found"; explaining each would be about
thirty model calls a run for no new meaning. Alternative: `explain` only lists the watch items
and non-findings with their stored reasons, which the dashboard already does.

**What this evidence does not establish:** how well the model keeps a watch item apart from a
finding in its wording; that is read in the answers, not measured here.

## Fix

- `explain.watch_items` gives the run's stage-3 gene-level watch items (`carrier.STAGE3_SOURCES`,
  no vid) in `query`'s finding shape, and `explain.to_explain` (the findings at the loosest
  filters, then those watch items) is what both `genokeeper explain` and `refresh --explain`
  ask for.
- `prompt` adds `WATCH_NOTE` to a watch item's instructions, before their last line, and its
  `kind` to its facts: the item is not a finding but a watch item, neither normal nor a
  finding, needing confirmation; every "finding" in the instructions reads as the watch item
  and the carrier example does not apply; never to be worded, in title or text, as a
  finding, an expansion, carrier status or a diagnosis. A finding's prompt is unchanged byte
  for byte (no `kind`, no note), so no cached explanation's key moves
  (`test_the_english_prompt_addresses_the_reader_and_the_russian_one_is_unchanged`, whose
  pinned keys still hold).
- The dashboard's stage-3 watch list shows a watch item's cached "what it means" under its
  call, with the model and the sources it cited (not its headline, which a finding's row
  leads with); a non-finding carries none.
- README: every line on what `explain` and `refresh --explain` send says that stage-3 watch
  items are sent and explained too, and the command's help says so.

Tests: `tests/test_explain.py::test_explain_asks_for_a_stage3_watch_item_as_a_watch_item`
(in English and Russian, a stage-3 watch item is asked with the note before the facts and
its kind in them; a stage-3 non-finding, another source's gene-level watch and a watch at a
variant are not; no finding's prompt carries the note or a kind),
`tests/test_dashboard.py::test_a_watch_items_cached_explanation_shown` and
`tests/test_refresh.py::test_a_failed_explain_call_fails_the_explain_step` (the refresh step
asks `to_explain`).

Not changed: stage-3 non-findings are not explained (their call and stored reason say "normal"
or "not found"), and neither is a watch item of any other source, which the dashboard does
not list either.
