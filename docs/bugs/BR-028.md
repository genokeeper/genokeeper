- **Found:** 2026-11-02, in the review of the SpliceAI renamed-genes fix.
- **Where:** SpliceAI 1.3.1's bundled `annotations/grch38.txt` (GENCODE v24), read by
  `annotate.vep.annotate` through SpliceAI's output; the `genome-ask` skill.

SpliceAI scores a variant only against genes whose span in its bundled annotation holds the
variant. Its PCDH15 model (chr10, minus strand, 53808069-54801291, 0-based start) ends in another
last exon (53808069-53809546) than MANE's: MANE Select ENST00000644397.2's exon 38, the last
coding exon, is chr10:53806581-53807130 (0-based start; 549 coding bases), wholly outside the
span. A splice change in that exon or at its acceptor gets no PCDH15 entry, so no SpliceAI score,
and never reaches the SpliceAI rule; a change at the acceptor's ±1/2 bases is still a VEP
`splice_acceptor_variant`, graded as a loss of function. A missed finding, never a false one.

**Evidence.** Every MANE CDS block of the 190 genes SpliceAI runs on (`coverage/cds.GRCh38.tsv`,
MANE Select and Plus Clinical; ELP1, G6PC1, GBA1 and MMUT under their old names) was checked
against its gene's span in SpliceAI's annotation, copied offline from the local SpliceAI image
(the annotation file's sha256 is 565c0ea83f66b2210182364c3d626fef526229e3d02f444bab247329b6695f2c):
of the 188 compared, PCDH15 is the only gene with coding bases outside its span (549 of its MANE
Select transcript's 5,220; its Plus Clinical transcript lies inside). PLP1 and SLC37A4 were not compared (no
annotation gene; no MANE row). `tests/test_skill_contract.py::test_skill_names_the_annotation_limits`
fails before the change: the skill never names it.

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** none without changing SpliceAI's bundled annotation (its `-A`
option takes another file, which changes every score and needs an annotation the lab does not
hold). Name the limit where a PCDH15 answer reaches it, the skill's Predicted findings. Must not
change: SpliceAI's inputs and the `vep` step's key.

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether a real run carries a variant in that exon.

## Fix

Rejected as a code fix, closed with the limit named: no change short of replacing SpliceAI's
bundled annotation (every score changes, and the lab holds no annotation to replace it with) gives
PCDH15's last coding exon a score. The skill's Predicted findings now say SpliceAI's 2016
annotation lacks PCDH15's last coding exon (MANE exon 38) and its splice acceptor, so a splice
change there gets no SpliceAI score, while a change at the acceptor's ±1/2 bases is still graded
as a loss of function. Covered by
`tests/test_skill_contract.py::test_skill_names_the_annotation_limits`. Not changed: SpliceAI's
inputs and the `vep` step's key.
