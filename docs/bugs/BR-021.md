- **Found:** 2026-11-02
- **Where:** `src/genokeeper/deletions/screen.py`, `is_common`; `src/genokeeper/carrier.py`,
  the `genotype --gene` summary.

gnomAD-SV v4.1 holds two common depth-only deletions over CBS's targets,
`gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr21_ff594b43` (18 kb, AF 0.215) and `gnomAD-SV_v3_DEL_chr21_67ca8d99`
(126 kb, AF 0.468). They lie over the real copy of a region GRCh38 also holds a false copy
of (chr21:5.0-7.9 Mb, which slice 3c masks for this pipeline's alignment, not gnomAD's), and
no homocystinuria allele is that common, so they are most likely artefacts of that
duplication. A true deletion over CBS that overlaps either by half or more both ways is
dropped as `COMMON` (as is a depth loss), and `genotype --gene CBS` says nothing of it.

**Evidence.** On `origin/main` at 8a88393, a hand-built 14 kb two-caller deletion over CBS's
exons against those two records (ids, spans and AF as the lab's gnomAD-SV file holds them;
a script, its output kept):

```
a 14 kb two-caller deletion over CBS exons: ({}, 'common in gnomAD-SV')
genotype --gene CBS keys naming a deletion limit: [] mechanism: None
```

**Suggested fix (a hypothesis):** name the limit where a CBS question reaches it: a line in
`genotype --gene CBS`, not a rule change. Must not change: the `COMMON` test. Alternative:
depth-only gnomAD-SV records never make a deletion called from split or paired reads
common; that changes three genes, not one (HBA1/HBA2's −α3.7 call would be shown where the
run states it not covered, and SMN1's beside slice 3a's copy number). Not the per-gene
mechanism table (`mechanism_not_assessed.tsv`): CBS's main mechanism is point variants, which
the run assesses, and that table reads "main mechanism not assessed".

**What this evidence does not establish:** whether the two records are artefacts; that
needs gnomAD's own alignment over the region.

## Fix

`screen.DELETION_SCREEN_LIMITS` maps a gene to what the deletion screens cannot see there,
with the two record ids and the artefact reasoning cited beside it; its one entry is CBS. The
overlap is reciprocal, half of both lengths, so only a call of about 9 kb or more can match
the 18 kb record (63 kb or more the 126 kb one); 3d's depth losses meet the same test, and
the line names both. `genotype --gene` returns it as `gene.deletion_screen_limit` (null
elsewhere) and prints `  deletion screen limit: …` after the mechanism line; the README
lists it beside the mechanism. The `genome-ask` skill says to word it as "the screens can
miss a large deletion in CBS", never as a doubt on the gene's other answers or on a smaller
deletion, and the contract pins the field. Covered by
`tests/test_carrier.py::test_cbs_names_its_deletion_screen_limit` and
`tests/test_skill_contract.py` (the genotype contract).

Not changed: the `COMMON` test, the mechanism table, the dashboard (it has no per-gene panel
but the mechanism list).
