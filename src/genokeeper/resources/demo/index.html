Genokeeper takes the raw reads of a 30x genome and calls them on your own Mac. It grades each finding twice: how likely the genotype is wrong, measured on reference genomes, and how strong the evidence behind the claim is. This page is its output for HG002.

Ask it yourself

With Claude Code installed, three steps put this genome in a folder you can ask questions about. It downloads the store from GitHub and sends nothing.

  1. Get the folder.uvx genokeeper demo
  2. Open it.cd ~/genokeeper-demo && claude
  3. Ask.Is this person a carrier of anything their children could inherit?

The answers go through Claude, so the findings a question needs are sent to Anthropic. For HG002 that is public data. For your own genome it is your choice.

What it cannot do

  • It reads only paired FASTQ from a DNBSEQ-T7 run of about 30x. Illumina reads are refused.
  • Your own run needs an Apple silicon Mac with 48 GB, about 1 TB of free disk and several days.
  • It is not a medical device. A finding is a reason to repeat the test in a clinical lab.
